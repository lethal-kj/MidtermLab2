El Nido, Palawan

El Nido is famous for its towering limestone karsts and turquoise lagoons best explored by island-hopping boat tours. Sunset views from Las Cabanas Beach are a local favorite.

Boracay White Beach

Boracay's White Beach stretches four kilometers of soft, powdery sand facing the sunset. Station 1 is the quietest stretch, ideal for early morning walks.

Chocolate Hills, Bohol

These grass-covered limestone mounds turn brown in the dry season, giving the hills their chocolate-like appearance. The Sagbayan Peak viewpoint offers the widest panorama.

Banaue Rice Terraces

Carved into the mountains by the Ifugao people, these terraces are often called the 'Stairway to Heaven.' They remain hand-tended using centuries-old irrigation methods.

Mayon Volcano

Mayon's near-symmetrical cone makes it one of the most photographed volcanoes in the world. The Cagsawa Ruins at its base offer a classic framing shot.

Vigan Heritage Village

Calle Crisologo is lined with preserved Spanish colonial houses, best explored on foot or by kalesa (horse-drawn carriage) in the early evening.

Siargao, Cloud 9

Cloud 9's reef break draws surfers from around the world. Beyond the waves, Siargao offers island hopping to Naked Island and Guyam Island.

Sagada

Sagada is known for its centuries-old hanging coffin burial tradition, limestone caves, and the Echo Valley viewpoint above Lumiang Cave.

Coron Island

Coron's Kayangan Lake is often ranked among the cleanest lakes in Asia. The surrounding waters also hold several Japanese WWII shipwrecks popular with divers.

Batanes Rolling Hills

Batanes' rolling hills and stone houses give it a landscape unlike anywhere else in the country, shaped by frequent typhoons and strong winds.

Baguio Pine Forest

Baguio's cool climate and pine-covered hills make it a favorite highland escape, with Camp John Hay and Mines View Park among its best-known spots.

Eden Nature Park, Davao

Eden Nature Park sits on a reforested mountain resort offering cool weather, garden trails, and a wide view of Davao City below.
